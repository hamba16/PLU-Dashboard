Trust and methodologyHow the public tracker worksRead methodology +

This register brings together public-interest allegations and official developments. Publication records a matter for accountability; it does not establish guilt.

01

Source classification

Entries identify the source used, including official records, court material, audit reports, credible media reporting or a public allegation awaiting confirmation.

02

Review and updates

Case stages and timelines are updated when a traceable public source records a meaningful development.

03

Amount terminology

Amount under review means the value cited in the published allegation or source. It is not automatically a confirmed loss or a recovered sum.

04

Right of reply / correction request

Corrections and additional public records can be submitted for editorial review.
