Chairman - Patriotic League of Uganda

Welcome to the Patriotic League of Uganda

Gen. Muhoozi Kainerugaba

It is my pleasure to invite every patriotic Ugandan to join us in the shared work of building a united, secure and prosperous country.

At the heart of our mission is a simple conviction: Uganda's national interest must never be outsourced. Our sovereignty, security and national narrative must be shaped by Ugandans committed to the country's progress.

General Secretary - Patriotic League of Uganda

Message from the General Secretary

Hon. Fadil Twalla

Dear Patriots, I warmly welcome you to the Patriotic League of Uganda. PLU is a home for Ugandans who believe that citizenship carries both pride and responsibility. It brings together people from every part of our country who are determined to protect Uganda’s achievements, strengthen national unity and contribute to the next chapter of our development. Our country has travelled a remarkable journey. The peace, stability and progress we enjoy today were made possible by the courage, sacrifice and commitment of Ugandans who chose to place the nation above themselves. PLU recognizes that legacy and calls upon the present generation to preserve it, build upon it and pass on a stronger Uganda.

Urgent announcement

Demonstration at the Turkish Embassy

As directed by the Chairman of the Patriotic League of Uganda, PLU will hold a full-force demonstration at the Turkish Embassy on 18 September 2026.

All concerned PLU structures, leaders, coordinators and supporters are expected to take note of the directive and prepare accordingly. The demonstration will be conducted under the designated leadership, with emphasis on proper coordination, discipline and a strong PLU presence.

Rt. Hon Fadil Twalla - General Secretary
